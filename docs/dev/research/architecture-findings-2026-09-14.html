Main findings

  • The modules are real. No two modules co-change in more than 20 percent of their commits. The folder and name boundaries match how the code is edited.
  • The pain is three files. ParsekFlight.cs (27,591 lines, in 1,125 of 5,127 commits, on one side of every top cross-module co-change pair), RecordingStore.cs (408 commits, 37 references into the cycle), Recording.cs (top hotspot: 132 commits times fan-in 162).
  • One cycle of 391 types spans every module, held by four references: the logger reading settings, and the recording data type calling its store and the crew ledger. Cutting those frees 110 types. The 253 left after the third cut are design work.
  • Parsek is data plus functions, not objects. 26 interfaces and 6 abstract bases in 1,335 types; 497 data types, 343 static helper classes. The abstractions are the 18 hub names below, not a class hierarchy.
  • The old Core was a filing error. 123 root-level files; 115 had an obvious owner. The real kernel is 8 files every module uses.
  • Trajectory is the pure module the docs claim once five recorder-side files stopped matching its name prefixes: 13 files, instability 0.06, six upward references.
  • Missions and Reaim are not one module. 21 references each way but co-change in only 15 percent of their commits: cut with an interface, do not merge.

Tooling

  • python scripts/arch/archview.py --check --place regenerates everything in about 20 s: module edges, per-type roles and levels, the cycle and its cuts, git co-change, the map, the matrix, the explorer, the type ladder, the atlas, the placement report. Generated files are gitignored.
  • Hand-authored inputs: scripts/arch/modules.toml (file to module, forbidden edges) and scripts/arch/atlas.toml (prose). Staleness in prose is reported by --check.
  • Reference edges come from a text scan (identifiers matched to declared type names after stripping comments, strings and directives). Names declared in two modules are dropped; method calls that share a type name are skipped. Good enough to place files and rank couplings; not sound enough to gate a build. A Roslyn extractor (phase 5) would replace the edges and keep every view.
